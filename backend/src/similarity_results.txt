Metric Justification
====================

Cosine similarity was chosen because it compares the direction
of embedding vectors.

For semantic embeddings, vector direction represents meaning
in the embedding space. Higher cosine similarity indicates
greater semantic relatedness.

Similarity and distance express the same retrieval idea from
different perspectives:

- Similarity: higher score means more related.
- Distance: lower score means more related.

The ranked results can be used to select the top-k chunks
that will later be provided to the RAG language model as
supporting context.

A high similarity score indicates semantic closeness, but it
does not guarantee that the chunk is factually correct,
complete, current, or safe to use by itself.
